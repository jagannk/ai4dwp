Title: Cannot Sign In - Help You Can Do Yourself
Version: v 1.0
Date: 07/08/2026
Status: Draft

If you are locked out or cannot sign in, do not worry. Your files and work data are safe.

Follow these steps in order:

1. Stop entering your password for 2 minutes.
What success looks like: You are calm and ready to try once carefully.

2. Check that the Caps Lock key is off (the key that makes letters ALL CAPITAL), then type your password once, slowly.
What success looks like: You sign in.

3. If sign-in fails again, wait 15 minutes without trying again.
What success looks like: You avoid a longer lockout and can try again after the wait.

4. After 15 minutes, try signing in one time only.
What success looks like: You sign in, or you get the same error.

5. If you recently changed your password, sign out of all other work computers and work programs, then sign in again on the computer you are using now.
What success looks like: You can sign in and stay signed in.

6. Restart your computer once if you still cannot sign in.
What success looks like: Sign-in screen returns and you can try again.

7. If the issue continues, contact the Service Desk.
What success looks like: Your support request is created with the right details and help can act quickly.

Details to include in your support request:
- Your full name and work login name
- Computer name (if shown on login screen or company sticker)
- Exact time the issue started
- Exact message shown on screen
- Whether you recently changed your password (Yes/No)
- Whether this happens on one computer or more than one
- Best phone number to reach you now

Contact the Service Desk if you still cannot sign in after the 15-minute wait and one restart.
