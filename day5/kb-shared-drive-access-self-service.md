Title: Cannot Access Finance Shared Drive - Help You Can Do Yourself
Version: v 1.0
Date: 10/08/2026
Status: Draft

If you cannot open the Finance shared drive (for example, the F: drive or Finance folder), do not worry. Your files are safe on the server. This is usually a connection or permissions issue that can be fixed quickly.

Follow these steps in order:

1. Check whether other people in your team also cannot access the drive.
What success looks like: You know whether this affects just you or the whole team. Tell the Service Desk this when you call.

2. Check your internet and network connection. Look at the network icon in the bottom-right corner of your screen. If it shows a red X or a warning sign, report this to the Service Desk straight away.
What success looks like: Your network icon shows connected (no red X or warning).

3. Lock your computer and sign back in (press Windows key + L, then sign in with your work password).
What success looks like: You are back at your desktop.

4. Try to open the shared drive again by clicking the drive letter in File Explorer (for example, F: or a Finance folder under This PC).
What success looks like: The drive opens and you can see files.

5. If the drive letter is missing from File Explorer, try typing the server path directly. Click the address bar in File Explorer and type:
   \\finance-server\Finance
   then press Enter.
What success looks like: A folder opens showing Finance files.

6. If you get a message saying "You do not have permission" or "Access denied", do not try again multiple times. This means your access may need to be checked by IT.
What success looks like: You stop here and contact the Service Desk with the exact message shown on screen.

7. Restart your computer once if the drive is still missing or will not open.
What success looks like: The drive letter reappears in File Explorer after restart and you can open it.

8. If the issue continues after restart, contact the Service Desk.
What success looks like: Your support request is created with the right details and help can act quickly.

Details to include in your support request:
- Your full name and work login name
- Computer name (shown on login screen or company sticker)
- Exact time the issue started
- Exact error message shown on screen
- Whether the drive letter is missing or shows but gives an error
- Whether other team members are also affected (Yes/No)
- Whether you recently changed your password or your account was locked out (Yes/No)
- Best phone number to reach you now

Contact the Service Desk if the drive is still unavailable after one restart.
