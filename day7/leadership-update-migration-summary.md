# Leadership Update – FinBridge Win11 Migration: End-User Experience Summary

**Prepared for:** Senior Leadership  
**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Prepared by:** DWP Analyst  

---

The Windows 11 migration has landed well for the majority of FinBridge staff. The issues that caused the most disruption in the first week — colleagues being locked out of their accounts, OneDrive files appearing missing, VPN dropping during calls, and slow login times — have all been resolved. Staff are actively acknowledging the improvement, and positive feedback has increased week on week. For most of the business, day-to-day working is back to normal and in several areas noticeably faster than before the migration.

Two issues remain open and are being actively worked. The floor 3 printer has not mapped automatically since the migration began, and despite a temporary workaround the team is using, this has now been unresolved for two weeks and confidence is low without a firm fix date — this will be escalated if not resolved imminently. Separately, Finance staff have begun reporting that Excel is crashing when working with large files, which is causing lost work and is the team's most pressing concern right now. In addition, Finance colleagues have been experiencing slower login times since a security update was applied last week; investigation is underway and early indications point to a configuration adjustment that can be made without rolling back the security improvement. Both issues are in active investigation and updates will follow as soon as root causes are confirmed.

---

*Based on: 90 end-user feedback comments (weeks 1–2) and login performance monitoring data*
