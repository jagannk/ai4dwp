## Ranking Methodology

Themes were ranked by weighting **both impact (Blocker / Friction / Minor / Positive) and volume (comment count)**, in that order.  
A single Blocker outranks many Minor comments. Within the Blocker tier, the **nature of the Blocker** (reversibility, workaround availability, data-integrity risk) takes precedence over raw count.

## Top 3 Themes – Act Today

---

### Rank 1 — OneDrive Missing Files

- **Count:** 4 comments (ids: 14, 23, 34, 42)
- **Severity:** Blocker
- **Why it ranks first:** Carries the highest-stakes risk of all themes: potential **data loss**. Unlike every other Blocker here, this issue may be irreversible — if files are gone rather than unsynced, no IT action can recover them after the fact. Users are citing same-day Finance deadlines. Count is lower than Printer or Lockout, but the consequence of inaction is categorically worse.
- **Representative quotes:**
  - *"My OneDrive files are missing! This is urgent, need them for a meeting."* (id:14)
  - *"Missing files in OneDrive -- checked three times, still not there."* (id:42)
- **Manager one-liner:** "Four Finance staff are reporting missing OneDrive files with same-day deadlines — we need to confirm whether this is a sync delay or data loss before end of business."

---

### Rank 2 — Account Lockout

- **Count:** 5 comments (ids: 11, 16, 29, 37, 45)
- **Severity:** Blocker
- **Why it ranks second:** Complete work stoppage with **no workaround** — a locked account cannot be bypassed. Recurrence (multiple users locked out 2–3 times in the same week) signals a systemic policy misconfiguration, not user error, meaning it will keep happening without a fix. At least one lockout occurred immediately before a client call, creating reputational risk.
- **Representative quotes:**
  - *"Locked out of my account, IT hasn't called back yet, 2 hours now."* (id:11)
  - *"Account lockout happened right before an important client call."* (id:45)
- **Manager one-liner:** "At least five users have been locked out repeatedly this week, including immediately before client calls, suggesting a policy misconfiguration that needs urgent review."

---

### Rank 3 — Floor 3 Printer Not Mapping

- **Count:** 6 comments (ids: 3, 13, 19, 26, 35, 43)
- **Severity:** Blocker
- **Why it ranks third:** Highest comment volume of all themes but **demoted from rank 1** because a workaround exists — staff are already walking to floor 2 to print. The issue is disruptive and overdue for resolution, but it is not paralysing. It ranks above deferred Blockers (Shared Drive, AVD) due to volume and multi-day persistence.
- **Representative quotes:**
  - *"Printer on floor 3 -- team has given up and is walking to floor 2 to print."* (id:43)
  - *"Printer floor 3 -- can we please get an ETA, client meeting at 2pm."* (id:35)
- **Manager one-liner:** "Six staff on floor 3 have been unable to print client documents for multiple days and are walking to floor 2 as a workaround — we need a fix or ETA today."
