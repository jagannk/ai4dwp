# FinBridge Migration – Self-Service Help (Top 3 Issues)

---

## 1. My OneDrive Files Are Missing

First, wait 5 minutes and refresh your OneDrive folder — files sometimes take a little time to finish syncing after the migration.

If files are still missing, click the OneDrive cloud icon in your taskbar (bottom-right), then click **Sync now** or **Resume sync** if it appears.

**Still missing after trying this?**
Contact the service desk straight away — do not wait. Tell them: your name, which files or folders are missing, and the last time you successfully opened them. Mention it is urgent if you need the files today.

---

## 2. I've Been Locked Out of My Account

Your account locks automatically after a few wrong password attempts. To get back in, simply wait **15 minutes** and then try logging in again with your correct password.

If you have forgotten your password, go to the self-service password reset page your IT team provided and follow the steps there.

**Still locked out or keeps happening?**
Call the service desk directly (do not email — it is faster). Tell them: your name, your username, and roughly what time the lockout happened. Mention if this has happened more than once this week.

---

## 3. The Floor 3 Printer Isn't Showing Up

Try disconnecting and reconnecting to the network: click the Wi-Fi or network icon in the taskbar, disconnect, then reconnect. After that, restart your computer and check if the printer appears.

If it still does not appear, as a short-term workaround the printers on **floor 2 are available to use**.

**When to contact the service desk:**
If the printer has not appeared after restarting, log a ticket and include: your name, your computer name (shown at the bottom of the login screen), floor and desk location, and how many days the issue has been happening.

---

*End of self-service guide*
