We are aware that a number of colleagues are experiencing account lockouts this morning and are unable to log in. We want to reassure you that this is a known issue and our team is actively investigating it right now.

**In the meantime, please try this:**

If you have been locked out, wait 15 minutes and then try logging in again with your usual password. In many cases this will restore your access without needing to contact us.

If you have forgotten your password, use the self-service password reset link your IT team provided to regain access immediately.

**Please do not raise multiple tickets for the same issue** — this helps us keep our queue clear so we can resolve the root cause faster for everyone.

We will send a further update by **2:00 PM today**. If your access has not been restored by then or you have an urgent business need in the meantime, please call the service desk directly.

Thank you for your patience.
