- **Count:** 9 comments (ids: 4, 8, 13, 16, 20, 24, 30, 33, 37)
- **Severity:** Blocker
- **Pattern:** Excel crashes or freezes when opening large files, specifically flagged as files over 10MB and Finance budget spreadsheets. Users are losing unsaved work.
- **Affected group:** Finance team specifically called out; likely migration-related environment change (RAM, profile, app version).

> *"Excel keeps crashing, this is now my biggest issue with the new setup."* (id:30)  
> *"Excel crash is really disruptive, losing unsaved work each time."* (id:13)  
> *"New Excel crash issue -- happens specifically with files over 10MB."* (id:33)

**Action required:** This is the highest-priority new issue in W2. With 9 comments in a single week and Finance citing it as their biggest current problem, it warrants immediate investigation — likely a memory or compatibility issue introduced by the migration.
